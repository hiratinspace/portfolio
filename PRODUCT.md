# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers filling security, IT, and software internships and new-grad roles. They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, or a QR code, often skimming on a phone or between other candidates, and give the site well under a minute. Their job: decide whether Hirat is credible and hands-on, then open the resume or reach out.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Hirat Rahman Rahi (hiratrahi.com): a CS & Mathematics student (Psychology minor) at Illinois Wesleyan University focused on offensive security, who also founds and ships real products (REDHEXX, PassMyResume, TuitionCreep, interninbox, SpecterAI). Success: a recruiter leaves understanding Hirat's security focus, current IT/security internship, and shipped work, and clicks the resume or contact.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, credible, technical. A confident security engineer who is clearly hands-on: precise, no fluff, evidence over adjectives. The cybersecurity identity (dark, red, terminal cues) is part of the brand, but in service of credibility.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI or template portfolio scaffolding (identical card grids, eyebrow-on-every-section, stock hero formula).
- Corporate and bland: losing the cybersecurity edge and personality.
- Cluttered: too many sections, effects, or widgets competing for attention.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over claims: show shipped products, live data, and concrete numbers instead of adjectives.
2. Recruiter in 30 seconds: role, focus, and the resume/contact path must be obvious immediately, on mobile first.
3. Practice what you preach: a security person's site should itself be fast, accessible, and carefully built.
4. Edge with restraint: keep the red-team identity, but every effect must earn its place.
5. One accurate story: site content stays in sync with the current resume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: body text contrast at least 4.5:1, full keyboard access, visible focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives for the background and entrance animations.
